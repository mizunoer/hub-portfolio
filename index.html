A home AI fleet that runs itself

Dallin's home fleet: GPU machines on a private network, one hub in front of them that labels every request, routes it to the best local model (or a free external one when the content allows), remembers what matters, proposes its own model switches with evidence, and checks itself every day. This page is a read-only snapshot refreshed on a timer; nothing on it can reach the hub.
